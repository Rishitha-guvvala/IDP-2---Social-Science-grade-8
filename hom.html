🧠 Habits of Mind — Our Cognitive Pathways

Throughout the project, we used different Habits of Mind to guide how we researched, analysed information, developed ideas and communicated our solution.

Thinking Flexibly — We explored different ways of improving textile production and developed DyeCycle as an innovative approach to water treatment and reuse.

Managing Impulsivity — We considered the effects of material, water and chemical consumption before proposing more responsible textile-production practices.

Gathering Data Through All Senses — We researched, observed and analysed information about how textile industries can affect their surrounding environment.

Creating, Imagining, Innovating — We used creative thinking to develop DyeCycle, our proposed solution for more sustainable textile production.

Listening with Understanding and Empathy — We considered different perspectives and used communication to share our ideas and advocate for sustainable change.

🌍 Sustainable Development Goals

The United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are global goals designed to address major social, economic and environmental challenges and promote a more sustainable future.

SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure
Our DyeCycle proposal encourages innovation in textile production and water treatment.

SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities
Sustainable textile practices can help reduce environmental pressure on communities and their surrounding resources.

SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption and Production
Our project focuses on reducing unnecessary water and chemical use and encouraging more responsible production.

SDG 13 — Climate Action
Improving resource efficiency and reducing environmental pressure supports action towards a more sustainable future.

CONCLUSION

Re-engineering the Textile Industry
for a Sustainable Future

What I learned, developed and discovered through my research.

The textile industry is an important part of everyday life, but through my research, I found that textile production can also create environmental challenges, especially through high water use and wastewater from dyeing. This made me ask an important question: Can textiles be produced while using resources more responsibly?

From my research, I learned that textile dyeing can use large amounts of water, and wastewater can contain dyes, chemicals and other substances. I also learned that wastewater can be treated using processes such as coagulation and filtration. In coagulation, small suspended particles can join together to form larger groups called flocs, which can then be separated more easily.

Based on my research, I developed my idea of DyeCycle. The idea is to collect used water from textile processes, treat it to remove unwanted substances, and then check whether the treated water is suitable for reuse in appropriate textile processes. This can help reduce the need for fresh water and encourage more responsible use of resources.

I also used Maths to understand the problem through data. My Excel analysis compared regular and cationic dyeing across six samples. The research data showed lower water consumption for cationic dyeing in all six samples, with reductions ranging from about 20% to 36%.

This project also helped me connect my research with HOM and the SDGs. I used flexible thinking, gathered and analysed information, and created a solution based on my findings. My project connects particularly with goals related to clean water, sustainable industry, responsible production and climate action.

Most importantly, this project showed me that solving a real-world problem does not always mean finding one perfect answer. It means researching the problem, understanding the evidence, thinking of possible solutions, using data to support ideas, and communicating them clearly.

As a responsible citizen, I believe I should think about the impact of the products I use and encourage industries to use resources more carefully. If textile production can move towards better water management, treatment and reuse, I believe this can help create a more sustainable textile industry.

DyeCycle is my proposed idea, but the bigger message is simple: use resources wisely, reduce waste, and make every part of production more sustainable.

When water and chemicals leave the factory, where do they go?

Textile wet-processing stages such as bleaching, dyeing, printing and finishing generate wastewater. If this wastewater is not properly treated, pollutants can enter water bodies, affecting aquatic ecosystems and people who depend on those water resources.

The scale of the issue is significant. UNEP estimates that the textile industry consumes around 215 trillion litres of water each year, while textile production also uses substantial quantities of chemicals.

🌍 Three Areas of Impact

🌊 Environmental
Water pollution can threaten aquatic life and ecosystems.

👥 Social
Communities and other users who depend on local water resources can be affected when water quality or availability is reduced.

💰 Economic
Water pollution and resource pressure can create additional costs for treatment, water supply and cleaner production.
